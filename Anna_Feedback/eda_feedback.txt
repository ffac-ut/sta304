Chi-square test

The way you describe the chi-square test is awkward. You note that 11 of 15 expected counts are below 5, that the minimum is 0.083, and that the expected-count requirement is substantially violated. You then say the p-value cannot support a reliable significance claim and that no alternative test is introduced. I permit proceeding with the planned analysis when an assumption is violated, so my concern is the wording and not the test itself. When you notice a violation, my Hypothesis Testing & Statistical Tests Review document (https://annahuynhly.github.io/pages/teaching/STA304/Review.pdf) explains what you can do, which is Fisher's exact test as the nonparametric alternative. I would prefer you use it.

ANOVA
