Chi-square test of independence
Please do not use the chi-square test of independence to test whether study break frequency is independent of perceived study efficiency. This test is for two categorical variables, and both of yours are quantitative, so it is not appropriate here.

Course-specific ID
Please make the course-specific ID question mandatory. I need to report this value as reliably as possible so that every student gets the marks for completing surveys.

Ranges and numeric answers
Many of your questions give predetermined ranges where you could ask for a number. For example, ask students how long they study in minutes or hours, and ask for the number of study hours directly. The same goes for the length of a study break. Ranges throw away information that would help your quantitative analysis.

Rating scales
Some of your questions use a 1 to 5 scale. Please widen these to 1 to 7 or 1 to 10.
Your statement about feelings uses a strongly agree to strongly disagree scale. Please add "slightly agree" and "slightly disagree" to give respondents more options. Larger scales work better for quantitative analysis.

Types of study breaks
Snapchat is an unusual category for study breaks, since students are more likely to use Instagram or TikTok. Consider replacing it with a general "social media" option. You already have an "other" option, which helps.
